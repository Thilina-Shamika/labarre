About collecting

We have specialized in collectible stocks and bonds for decades — offering a great way to start your own collection, or even your own business. The hobby is now known the world over as Scripophily.

Stocks and bonds have appeared on national TV, in newspapers and magazines, and in the movies. Enthusiasm for the field first took hold in Europe, and American demand has grown steadily ever since — a rich, still-young corner of collecting.

Recommended reading: The Scripophily Guide by Howard Shakespeare.

Why Collect

Six reasons collectors are hooked

History

Pieces of the past

Financial history is exciting and fun. Stocks and bonds financed colonial times, early shipping, the railroads, the Industrial Revolution, the automotive age, the Crash of 1929, and every decade of growth since. Nearly everyone has a relative who worked for a great company or on the railroad — this history is truly educational and a joy to hold.

Art

Engraved masterworks

The engraved printing of American Bank Note, Franklin-Lee, International Bank Note and others is considered serious art. The same firms printed the world’s paper money. These certificates decorate dens, offices, boardrooms, and corporate headquarters — and make wonderful educational tools.

The Hunt

Treasure-hunting appeal

Because collecting stocks and bonds is still so new, one might say it is “still in its infancy.” There are bargains and little treasures waiting to be found — a fresh, vibrant hobby with room to discover.

Value

Strong, proven value

Back in 1981, the most valuable collectible stock sold for $3,000; that same railroad stock signed by Commodore Cornelius Vanderbilt now sells for around $12,000, with records reaching $130,000. Next to coins, stamps, and autographs — which reach into the millions — quality collectible stocks and bonds represent very good value today.

Rarity

Increasingly scarce

Genuine pieces are becoming difficult to find. Most of our inventory was acquired 10 to 25 years ago, and demand has climbed as supply has fallen. Most companies no longer issue paper certificates at all — so this is a rare chance to enter a collectible world for very little money.

Variety

Something for everyone

Railroads, automotive, aviation, mining, oils, communications, insurance, banking, autographs — and certificates can be collected by state, city, or region. Every taste is served.

Most importantly — collecting stocks and bonds is fun, and deeply satisfying.
