07 · Signature Treatment

Matting & the Frame

Every item photograph is presented like a gallery piece: a white mat, a thin inner hairline inset, and a soft cast shadow. This is the house style use it for the hero and any feature image.

Item scan
  • Outer mat — 18px white padding, 1px --line-2 border.
  • Inner inset — a 1px --line rule floated 9px inside, drawn with ::after.
  • Shadow — 0 30px 70px -34px rgba(31,39,56,.45), soft and low.
  • Caption — italic Garamond title left, mono lot number right.

12 · Usage

Do & Don't

The guardrails that keep the storefront feeling like a single, considered gallery.

Do
  • Use gold as a single accent links, eyebrows, one CTA.
  • Mat every feature image with the white frame treatment.
  • Use the darker tan #5F5746 for all listing copy.
  • Let borders and hairlines carry structure before shadows.
Don't
  • Use oxblood for anything but live auction urgency.
  • Round corners or add glossy / colored drop shadows.
  • Set listing descriptions in the light label tan.
  • Introduce new typefaces or bright, saturated colors.
