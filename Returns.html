Returns

If a piece isn’t right for you, returning it is simple and fair.

Ten-day inspection

Items may be returned within ten days of receipt for a full refund of the purchase price, provided they are returned in the same condition, in the original packaging.

Our authenticity guarantee

All autographed items are unconditionally guaranteed to be authentic — without time limit — to the original purchaser. This guarantee stands for as long as you own the piece.

How to return

  • Write or call before returning so we can expect the package.
  • Pack the item exactly as it arrived.
  • Ship insured; refunds are issued once the piece is received and inspected.
