Shipping

Every order is packed by hand and insured in transit. Here’s what to expect.

Free U.S. Shipping Over $500

Domestic orders totaling $500 or more ship free. Smaller orders ship at a modest flat rate, calculated at checkout.

How We Pack

Documents are sleeved in archival mylar, sandwiched between rigid boards, and sent in a reinforced flat mailer or box. Larger and framed pieces are custom-packed.

Transit & Insurance

  • Orders ship within 1–2 business days of cleared payment.
  • All shipments are fully insured for their purchase value.
  • Tracking is emailed as soon as your order leaves the gallery.

International Orders

We ship worldwide. International buyers are responsible for any duties or taxes; write to us for a shipping quote before ordering.
