The Piece

Description & Provenance

Issued in 1884 by the Broadway & Seventh Avenue Railroad Company — one of Manhattan’s great horse-car lines before the age of electric traction — this $1,000 mortgage bond carries a crisp, steel-engraved eagle vignette and the ornate border work that defines the finest Gilded-Age scripophily.

The certificate is uncancelled, with a partial sheet of coupons still attached, and survives in Fine condition with strong paper and clean, legible printing throughout. Pieces from this line rarely reach the market with coupons intact.

Why It Matters

The Broadway & Seventh Avenue line was among the most heavily trafficked street railways in the country, and its securities passed through the hands of the era’s most prominent financiers. A handsome, displayable document of New York’s transit history.
