Societies & Associations

Membership organizations

American Numismatic Association

The largest collector organization for coins and paper money in the United States.

www.money.org/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Home

International Bank Note Society

For anyone interested in foreign paper money. Members receive magazines, an auction catalog, and more — a must for the serious collector.

www.ibns.it/

The Society of Paper Money Collectors

Research, publications, and shows devoted to U.S. and world paper currency.

www.spmc.org/

Professional Currency Dealers Association

A dealer body upholding standards of practice in the currency trade.

www.pcdaonline.com/

The International Bond & Share Society

The worldwide society for scripophily — collectors of old stock and bond certificates.

www.scripophily.org/
