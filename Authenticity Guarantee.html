Authenticity Guarantee

The cornerstone of the gallery: everything we sell is exactly what we say it is.

Our promise

All autographed items are unconditionally guaranteed to be authentic, without time limit, to the original purchaser. If a piece is ever shown not to be genuine, we will refund the full purchase price.

How we authenticate

Every signature and certificate is examined against known exemplars and decades of hands-on experience. When appropriate, pieces are reviewed by recognized third-party authorities.

What you receive

  • A clear description of the item, its date, and its signatories.
  • Our lifetime guarantee of authenticity, in writing.
  • A specialist you can call with any question, for as long as you own the piece.
